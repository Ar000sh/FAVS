Why cut here? One person, six models (DDD slide 40)

Alice from "Mallorca 2027" is the same person in every context, but each context knows different things about Alice. Where the meaning changes, we draw a boundary. Contexts link Alice only by ID, never through one shared User class.

Identity

Alice is a User
  • account, email, password
  • display name "Alice"
Doesn't know: groups, expenses, money

Group

Alice is a Member (ADMIN)
  • created "Mallorca 2027" on day 0
  • co-members: Carol, David, Eve (joined day 3)
  • Bob: FORMER_MEMBER after day 5
  • closes the group on day 6
Doesn't know: who paid what

Expense

Alice is a Payer and a Participant
  • payer: Hotel €900, Farewell dinner €250
  • shares: 225 + 50 + 140 + 100 + 12 + 50 = €577
Doesn't know: Alice's role or password

Settlement

Alice is a Creditor
  • balance 1150 − 577 = +573
  • after Bob pays €202: +371
  • plan: David → Alice €371
Doesn't know: which expense was the hotel

Activity

Alice is an Actor
  • "Alice recorded Hotel €900"
  • "Alice closed the group for settlement"
Read only, owns no rules

Notification

Alice is a Recipient
  • "Bob paid you €202, please confirm"
Doesn't know: why the payment exists
